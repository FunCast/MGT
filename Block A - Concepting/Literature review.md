### Animation principles
##### Opening statement
Disney animators Ollie Johnston and Frank Thomas first introduced the principles of animation in their 1981 book _The Illusion of Life_. These principles include Squash and Stretch, Anticipation, Staging, Straight Ahead Action and Pose to Pose, Follow Through and Overlapping Action, Slow In and Slow Out, Arc, Secondary Action, Timing, Exaggeration, Solid Drawing, Appeal
##### Closing statement
These principles are old, but still commonly used and very much loved to this day. Setting a standard in the animation industry for us to keep up.
