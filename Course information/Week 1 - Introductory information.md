4 blocks of 8 weeks each (last one is 7). One continuous project.

A. Concepting - make it smaller, make it realistic
B. Pre-Production - How to collect data, test the [[Methodology]]
C. Production - Collecting data
D. Release - Release your [[Thesis]], write the discussion and conclusion. What is the answer to your research question and what does it mean for the industry. How do you present your results.

- Can it be answered in one year?
- Does it fill a real gap?
- Is it specific?
- Could the answer surprise you?
- Is it locked in forever?

Every block you deliver a (version of your) [[Thesis]], and a [[Learning log]]. The [[Thesis]] has the how and why. The [[Learning log]] is about whether you're managing planning, and how things are going with the progress. The [[Learning log]] needs to be exportable and readable as a .pdf or PowerPoint. Make it a weekly habit to log your work.
In addition you do a 15 min presentation every block. What do you need to prove? Make sure that's on the slides. (dates will be on the Team calendar)

You are free to use [[AI]], if you deem it a good idea. Think about how and why, the process of writing a thesis is a skill of its own. Have a [[AI]] use document. What did you use it for, what was the prompt, etc. If the [[AI]] makes a mistake, that's on you.
